# Code of Conduct

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## The short version

Be decent to people. You are here to talk about uploads, buckets and TypeScript; everyone should be able to do that
without being harassed, whoever they are, wherever they come from, and whatever they think of tabs.

## Do

- Treat people with respect, including the ones who disagree with you.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume anything else.
- Take feedback on your code as feedback on your code, not on you.
- Keep the discussion on what makes the project better, not on winning the argument.

## Don't

- Harass, troll, insult or personally attack anyone, in public or in private.
- Use sexualised language or imagery, or make unwelcome advances.
- Publish someone's private information without their permission.
- Do anything a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

Being blunt about code is fine. Being blunt about people is not.

## Scope

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and wherever someone represents the
project.

## Enforcement

Report a violation to **hello@vcode.sh**. Every report is read and handled in confidence.

Consequences match the behaviour: a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban. I would rather never use any of them.
